Inspect metal plates and handle quality incidents

Upload photos of metal plates. A trained model marks each plate as normal or defective. A Defective result can become a quality incident with actions cited from the product's SOPs, and a supervisor reviews it. Every result is saved.

  1. 1
    Engineers inspect one plate at a time or upload a batch of up to 10 images, and create quality incidents from Defective results.
  2. 2
    The model checks each image and gives a verdict with a confidence score.
  3. 3
    Supervisors review quality incidents and results, filter the history and see how the model was evaluated.
  4. 4
    Administrators register the products that can be inspected.
